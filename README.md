# Privacy-Policy
**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Abdelrhman Ahmed** ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Egypt, handles information when you use our mobile applications on iOS and Android (the "App").

By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the App.

**Purchases and subscriptions.** The App is a paid service. When you subscribe or purchase, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes the payment and tells the App whether you have an active subscription. We receive only your purchase and subscription status (such as product identifier and whether it is active or expired). We never receive or store your payment card details or billing address.

**Information stored on your device.** Any content you enter in the App is stored locally on your device. We do not receive or have access to it unless the App explicitly states otherwise.

**Support communications.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

**Device and diagnostic data.** We do not run our own servers that track you. Apple and Google may separately collect crash and diagnostic data under their own policies if you have enabled sharing with developers in your device settings.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

- To unlock and maintain access to paid features by verifying your subscription status.
- To respond to your support requests.
- To prevent unauthorized access to paid features.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

Purchases are handled by **Apple App Store In-App Purchase (StoreKit)** and **Google Play Billing**. These services process data under their own privacy policies:

- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not share your information with any other third parties.

---

## 4. Subscriptions and Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings (iOS) or Google Play subscription settings (Android). Deleting the App or your data does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Your Rights

We keep support emails only as long as needed to resolve your request, and subscription status records only as long as needed to provide access and meet legal obligations.

Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA), you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, email **synapselabssupport@gmail.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request" (or "Data Deletion Request" for deletion). We respond within 30 days.

Where GDPR applies, we process the limited data described above to perform our contract with you (providing paid access) and for our legitimate interests in supporting you and preventing fraud.

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at synapselabssupport@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
